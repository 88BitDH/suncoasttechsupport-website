Under Rule 2-18.002(2), place the following disclosure immediately next to the buyer's contract signature or **on the front page of the receipt** when no contract is used; print in **boldface type at least 10 points**. **Do not rely solely on a website policy link**. Under subsection (3), include the assignee warning below on applicable contract forms or notes.

**CONSUMER'S RIGHT OF CANCELLATION**  
**You may cancel this contract without any penalty or obligation within 3 business days from the above date, and receive a full refund of all payments made to the seller.**

**You may also cancel this contract if upon a doctor's order you cannot physically receive the services, or you may cancel the contract if the services cease to be offered as stated in the contract. If you cancel the contract for either of these reasons, the seller, 88 Bit Diamond Hands LLC doing business as Suncoast Tech Support, may keep only a portion of the contract price equal to a pro rata portion of the total price representing the proportion of services you used or completed, plus the cost to the seller of any related goods which you have consumed or retained.**

**Assignee warning — when included on a contract form or note (10-point bold, capital letters):**  
**THIS CONTRACT OR NOTE IS THE FUTURE CONSUMER SERVICES AND PUTS ALL ASSIGNEES ON NOTICE OF THE CONSUMER'S RIGHT TO CANCEL UNDER CHAPTER 2-18, F.A.C.**

**Refund handling:** For a qualifying cancellation under the disclosure, **issue a refund within 20 days after receipt of the customer's cancellation notice**. The Florida Attorney General notes that a future-services customer does not have to provide the cancellation notice in writing, although written proof is best practice. For a timely three-business-day cancellation, refund all payments. For a qualifying cancellation supported by a doctor's order or discontinuation of service, apply only the permitted pro-rata services-used and goods-consumed/retained deductions; do not apply the ordinary no-proration policy.

## Internal cancellation / refund checklist
1. Log original execution date, service type, amount, payment date, sales channel and customer's cancellation notice/date.
2. Determine whether Rule 2-18.002, home-solicitation statutes, or another consumer rule applies; preserve all overlapping protections.
3. For future services, confirm a completed, dated contract/receipt and the mandatory boldface notice were delivered at execution.
4. For qualifying home-solicitation sales, confirm signed written agreement and required notice, seller and employee contact details, and any permit requirements.
5. Record whether cancellation was within statutory cooling-off period, based on doctor's order, due to discontinued services, or an ordinary end-of-cycle cancellation.
6. For a timely future-services cancellation: full refund; **issue within 20 days of receiving valid notice**. For qualifying medical/service-unavailability claims: calculate lawful pro-rata services used and permitted retained goods costs.
7. For a qualifying home-solicitation cancellation: **tender refund and applicable evidence of indebtedness within 10 days after cancellation**.
8. Confirm subscription cancellation in Stripe; verify no further scheduled charges, and separately process/track required refunds (Stripe cancellation alone may not refund).
9. Send customer a dated written confirmation with amount refunded, date initiated and any remaining contracted service access. Record payment processor settlement separately from the statutory issue/tender deadline.
10. Audit no-rollover policy separately: **unused monthly onsite visit/minutes expire each billing period**, but this never cancels statutory refund rights or remedies when a promised service is not provided.

Residential Privacy Policy

Suncoast Tech Support is operated by 88 Bit Diamond Hands LLC, a Delaware limited liability company doing business in Florida as Suncoast Tech Support. Draft reviewed November 7, 2026; effective date is to be set upon approval and publication.

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our residential website, request or receive household technology support, purchase a monthly plan, or enroll in protection or third-party technology services. Our business-facing services may use separate documents or written agreements.

Summary: We use information to answer requests, provide services, schedule visits, administer purchased accounts, and process payments. Stripe handles payment-card entry during checkout. Additional providers such as Aura and Microsoft 365 process information needed for services you choose.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive:

  • Contact and scheduling information: your name, telephone number, email address, service address, preferred appointment times, and messages you send us.
  • Support and device information: information you provide about your computers, phones, printers, networks, operating systems, software, error messages, account permissions or technical issues, including notes or screenshots necessary to resolve a request.
  • Subscription and payment records: selected products, invoice amounts, payment status, renewal dates, and customer identifiers supplied by our payment provider. We do not need your full card number to process Stripe Checkout purchases.
  • Account-service information: information necessary to administer Microsoft 365, Aura, or other services you purchase from us, subject to the applicable provider and customer authorization.
  • Website and advertising interaction information: basic browser/device data, site pages, links used and events generated by analytics or advertising tools when enabled.

Please do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, bank credentials, or sensitive files through an ordinary contact form or email unless we have provided an appropriate secure method for the requested support.

2. Why we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, schedule and deliver visits, troubleshoot and document authorized support work, create or administer purchased subscriptions, process payments, notify you about account or security events, handle billing disputes or cancellations, maintain records, improve our website and meet legal obligations. We may contact you about services where permitted and will honor applicable opt-out choices.

3. Service providers and sharing

We share information only as reasonably needed for the services and activities described here, or when legally required. Service providers may include:

  • Stripe: secure payment processing, receipts, billing, subscriptions and customer portal access.
  • Microsoft: Microsoft 365 licenses, email, identity and account services when you have authorized them.
  • Aura: identity, privacy and scam-protection features for customers enrolled in Aura services; additional personal information may be submitted directly to Aura under Aura's own policies.
  • Zoho and other business communication tools: service requests, contact forms, email and support tickets.
  • Web hosting and analytics/advertising providers: operation, security, measurement and improvement of the website, including Google analytics or advertising tools where configured.
  • Approved contractors or specialist providers: only where needed to complete authorized work, with appropriate access controls.

We do not sell customer contact lists to data brokers. Certain measurement or advertising technologies may process website events under their own policies. Our providers may process information in other jurisdictions; we do not represent that all data remains exclusively within Florida or the United States.

4. Device access, support records and sensitive information

Technical support may require you to share logs, screenshots, configuration details or temporary access to devices and accounts. We limit intentional access to the scope of work you authorize and take reasonable steps to protect customer information, but no information system is risk-free. You remain responsible for maintaining appropriate independent backups unless we expressly agree to provide backup services. We aim to avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information and request secure communication methods when appropriate.

5. Website analytics, cookies and links

Our site may use analytics, advertising conversion tracking, necessary site storage, and links to external services. These tools can collect browser identifiers, device information, IP addresses, page interactions and approximate location or referral data according to their own settings. Browser privacy settings may allow you to limit cookies and tracking. Third-party sites reached by links are governed by those providers' privacy policies. We will review tracking practices and any additional consent or opt-out obligations as our site changes.

6. Service communications, email and text messages

We may send appointment confirmations, invoices, subscription notices, security alerts you request, and follow-up support communications. Where you have opted in to SMS communications, message frequency varies and carrier messaging/data charges may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of eligible automated or marketing text communications, and HELP for assistance where supported. Operational or individually requested support conversations may require you to contact us directly for changes. We do not sell SMS opt-in information to third parties for their unrelated marketing.

7. Retention and safeguards

We retain contact, service, payment and technical records as long as reasonably needed to fulfill services, resolve disputes, administer ongoing subscriptions, comply with accounting/legal obligations and protect our operations. Retention periods depend on the kind of record and applicable requirements. We use proportionate administrative and technical protections and limit access according to business needs. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices, privacy requests and contact

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also request changes to service communications or ask how a specific third party processes your information. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Customers may manage eligible Stripe subscriptions using the Suncoast billing portal.

Contact sales@suncoasttechsupport.com or call (727) 647-9000 for privacy inquiries. This policy may be revised as our services and providers change; the effective version will be posted here.

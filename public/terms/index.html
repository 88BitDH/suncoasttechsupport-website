6. Florida consumer cancellation notices and delivery requirements

Contracts for future continuing consumer services (Rule 2-18.002)

Where a residential subscription is a contract for consumer services to be delivered in the future on a continuing basis, Florida Administrative Code Rule 2-18.002 requires that the customer receive a fully completed, dated contract with the seller's name and address when it is executed. If no contract is used, the required notice must be on the front page of the receipt. The prescribed CONSUMER'S RIGHT OF CANCELLATION disclosure must appear adjacent to the contract signature or on the receipt's front page in bold type of at least 10 points. The notice states a right to cancel without penalty within three business days and obtain a full refund; it also explains cancellation when a doctor's order prevents physical receipt of services or the services cease to be offered as stated. In those latter circumstances the seller may retain only the legally permitted pro-rata portion for services used or completed and the cost of related goods consumed or retained. The rule requires refunds within 20 days after qualifying notice of cancellation. We will provide the required notice in the customer's transaction documents, not only on this website. There is no mandatory written-notice method for the future-services cooling-off right, although keeping written proof is recommended.

Qualifying home-solicitation transactions (Florida Statutes §§501.021–501.046)

If a sale meets Florida's definition of a home-solicitation sale, the customer has until midnight of the third business day after signing to cancel by the statutory written-notice methods. The signed agreement must carry the prescribed BUYER'S RIGHT TO CANCEL notice. Under §501.041, the seller must tender the buyer's payments and applicable evidence of indebtedness within 10 days of cancellation. The law distinguishes unsolicited solicitation from a sale resulting from a consumer's request for specific services; merely performing a requested home visit does not automatically make it a home-solicitation sale. If applicable, the required written agreement and cancellation notice will identify the seller's approved mailing address and the information required by the statute.

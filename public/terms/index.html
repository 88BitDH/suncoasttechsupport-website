3. Monthly technology support plans

Unless a different term is expressly agreed, our currently offered monthly support plans renew month to month. The package selected at checkout determines which benefits you receive:

These plans do not include unlimited onsite labor, all equipment, major network redesigns, extensive data recovery, emergency incident response or third-party fees unless specifically stated in writing. Additional labor and work will be quoted for approval. Included onsite visits are scheduled by mutual agreement; unused time and rollover, if any, depend on the purchased plan's written terms.

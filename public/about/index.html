About Suncoast Tech Support

Local technology help. Personal service.

Suncoast Tech Support provides practical technology assistance to homeowners, seniors and small businesses in Pinellas County and nearby Tampa Bay communities.

Suncoast Tech Support is a Florida OSD Certified Veteran-Owned Business, operated by 88 Bit Diamond Hands LLC.

Meet the founder

Ishmael “Moe” Muhammad is a U.S. Army veteran with more than 20 years of technology infrastructure and operations experience. His background includes work across financial services, healthcare, manufacturing and small-business IT.

He brings that experience to local customers who need clear explanations, dependable follow-through and support that fits their actual needs.

Why Suncoast exists

Technology support should be understandable and accessible. Our approach is to listen, explain the options in plain language and focus on a practical resolution—whether that means fixing a home computer, improving Wi-Fi, helping with data transfer or supporting a small office.

Who we help

Residential customers: Homeowners, families and seniors needing help with computers, printers, Wi-Fi, data transfers and security concerns.

Business customers: Local small businesses and organizations needing user support, Microsoft 365 assistance, networking and practical security services.

Serving Pinellas County and nearby communities

Suncoast supports customers in Clearwater, Largo, Dunedin, Safety Harbor, Palm Harbor, Seminole, St. Petersburg and surrounding Tampa Bay areas.

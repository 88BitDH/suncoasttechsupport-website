Common Home WiFi Coverage Issues

Many homes have areas where WiFi signal is weaker than expected. Walls, distance from the router, room layout, older equipment, and device location can all affect the quality of your connection.

We help review common coverage concerns and provide practical recommendations for improving your home WiFi experience.

Common signs of poor WiFi coverage include:

📶 Weak WiFi signal in the living room, bedroom, garage, patio, or home office
🚫 WiFi not reaching certain rooms
🐢 Slow internet in parts of the home
📺 Video buffering in rooms far from the router
🔌 Dropped connection in certain areas of the house
📱 Poor signal on phones, tablets, TVs, or laptops in specific rooms
📍 WiFi dead zones around the home
🏡 Coverage problems around the home
📡 Possible router placement concerns
🧱 Home layout affecting signal strength
